Notes

Add-ons

  • Standard can't buy any feature unlock. Its workspaces, social accounts, users, automation campaigns and storage are fixed by the plan. It can buy more API requests and AI credits.
  • Advanced can buy extra workspaces, users and social accounts, and Social Listening.
  • Agency Unlimited can buy extra social accounts, Social Listening, White-label and SAML SSO.
  • API requests can be topped up on any plan, 1,000 requests for $5.
  • Campaign & Label and Competitor Analytics can't be bought on these plans. The account flags that unlock them are set by hand.

Trials

  • Trials can't buy add-ons, and Social Listening is off on every trial.
  • Standard trial: as Standard, with fewer AI credits. Connecting Canva in Settings and the custom link shortener in the mobile app are also locked. The Canva button in the web composer still works.
  • Advanced trial: as Advanced, with fewer AI credits.
  • Agency Unlimited trial: fewer AI credits, capped workspaces and users, and no Looker Studio, dedicated onboarding, concierge setup, priority support or live training.

Where locks are enforced

  • The app's own backend doesn't check the plan for any feature. Locks live in the web app and the mobile app, which read the same plan flags. This applies to Classic plans too.
  • The mobile app locks the Inbox, approval workflows, EasyConnect, share links, grid views and Evergreen by plan. It doesn't block API-centric accounts from signing in.
  • The public API (and the CLI, MCP server, Zapier, Make and n8n on top of it) checks the plan for Ads, Competitor and Campaign & Label Analytics, the Inbox, Bulk CSV, RSS and Evergreen.
  • Approval workflows are not checked on the public API, so a Standard workspace can probably create them through the API or MCP server. Not tested.
  • API access is decided only by the plan's monthly API requests. The old api_access flag is still false on Standard, but nothing reads it.

Where the sources disagree

  • API-centric in the in-app comparison is stale. It says 5 social accounts, 10 GB and 10,000 API requests. The backend and the website's API pricing both say 10 accounts, 15 GB and 3,000 requests. It also marks publishing tools such as calendar, first comment, Canva, grid view, collab posts and shareable links as No, but the plan switches them on and the app shows them.
  • Content categories: the website's comparison marks Standard as No, but nothing in the app locks them on any plan.
  • Curated content automation: the in-app comparison advertises it on Advanced and Agency Unlimited. The website no longer lists it, and the app has no such page.
  • Card-trial plans (-cc), per code: the Advanced and Agency Unlimited plans for card-first signups were created without the looker_studio and Social Listening flags, so Looker Studio and Social Listening would show as locked for those paying customers. Needs checking in the live database.
  • Mobile apps on API-centric: the in-app comparison says No, but nothing in the mobile app blocks API-centric accounts.

Notes

Across all Classic plans

  • Classic plans have much more than their price suggests. Every one, Starter and lifetime deals included, has approval workflows, Campaign & Label Analytics, PDF reports, white-label reports, EasyConnect and client management, and can buy White-label and SAML SSO.
  • Ads Analytics is on every Classic plan except Starter.
  • Social Listening can be bought only on Business & Agency, the ContentStudio Agency plans and lifetime deals (5 topics, 10,000 mentions, per code).
  • X posting on lifetime deals runs through the pay-per-use X wallet. The plans themselves have X switched off.

How Classic billing differs

  • Classic recurring plans buy add-ons through the old Classic checkout. Only these accounts are offered Competitor Analytics as an add-on.
  • Lifetime deals buy add-ons through a separate Paddle Billing add-ons subscription. They can now buy the Inbox.
  • Classic subscribers can't change plan themselves. The app sends them to support, because Paddle Billing can't move a Classic subscription.
  • Old lifetime add-ons (Inbox, Competitor Analytics, Influencers) are no longer sold. Customers who bought them keep them.
  • Old per-account add-ons still unlock features: team members (team and approvals), automations (Evergreen, RSS, Bulk CSV), Bulk CSV, grid view and collab posts. Nothing sells them any more.
  • AppSumo stacked codes multiply every limit on the plan.

Where the 2024 pricing page disagrees

  • Starter was sold without approval workflows, team members, PDF reports or white-label reports. The plan has all four switched on. Starter Annual can invite team members; Starter Monthly can't.
  • Client management was listed on Agency only. Every Classic plan has it.
  • Users on Business & Agency: the page said 5 users on Agency Small. The plan allows the owner plus 5 members, so 6 people.
  • Curated content automation was sold on Pro and Agency. The app no longer has it.
  • AI credits were raised in May 2025 on Starter, Pro and Business & Agency. Growth was left out of that update.
