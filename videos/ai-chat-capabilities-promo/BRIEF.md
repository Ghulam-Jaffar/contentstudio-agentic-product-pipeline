## Intent

A 60 second capability tour of ContentStudio AI Chat (web), the same angle as the existing
`videos/ai-chat-promo`, rebuilt from scratch with the HyperFrames product-launch workflow so the
team can compare what the new tooling produces. Confident, clean SaaS promo.
