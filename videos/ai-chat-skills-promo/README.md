## Caveats

- The screens are styled mock-ups, not product screenshots. The menu group names come from `contentstudio-frontend/src/locales/en/ai_skills.json`.
- The primary blue (`#1f6bff`) is approximate. The exact brand value wasn't found in the frontend source, so check it against the brand guide.
- The report use case was left out on purpose. Chat can show a report but can't download or schedule one.
